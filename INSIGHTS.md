# Benchmark insights

[Open the current, snapshot-derived insights](https://www.nexus-index.com/leaderboard/current/insights).

The website calculates insight figures from the same versioned snapshots used by
the Benchmark Explorer. It shows the collection date, content type, publisher
population, matched era comparison, dimension profile, and linked publisher
examples. This file deliberately does not duplicate changing counts or ranks.

## Interpretation

- Current means publication/issue dates in the snapshot's current calendar year
  and the preceding two years. Overall covers all years.
- Aggregate indices combine only supported Crossref Participation Report work
  types, using work counts within each publisher. Publisher averages give each
  publisher equal weight.
- Organizations uses **affiliation-specific ROR coverage**. Funder ROR IDs are
  shown separately and do not count as affiliations.
- Funding includes the union of existing funder metadata and funder ROR IDs,
  counting each work once. The weights remain 10 points for funder information
  and 10 for award numbers. It is not a measure of how much research was funded.
  Marginal identifier percentages are never simply added together.
- A Current-versus-Overall difference compares publication cohorts within one
  snapshot. It does not establish improvement over time or explain a cause.
- Unsupported schemas and incomplete required coverage are not assigned a zero
  or an F. An explicitly reported zero remains an observed zero.
- The index measures metadata visible in Crossref, not publisher or research
  quality. Crossref and other sources can enrich deposited records.

See [benchmark methodology](docs/benchmark-methodology.md) and
[refresh automation](docs/benchmark-automation.md). Changes to scoring weights,
unfunded research treatment, and institutional authorship remain community
methodology questions; this update does not change weights or grade thresholds.
